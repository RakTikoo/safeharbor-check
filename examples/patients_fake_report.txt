WHAT THIS IS: a mechanical checker for structured CSVs against the
identifier classes in 45 CFR 164.514(b)(2). It is a checker only.
A clean result here does not certify that a file meets HIPAA
requirements. Research/educational tool -- not a medical device,
not legal advice.

RESIDUAL RISK -- READ THIS:
  * Free-text columns cannot be verified by this tool. A person can
    type a name, a date, or any other identifier into a notes field,
    and no pattern scan can prove they did not. Free-text columns
    are always reported as CANNOT VERIFY -- manual review required,
    and are never reported as passing.
  * Class (R) -- any other unique identifying number, characteristic
    or code -- cannot be verified mechanically. Only a human who
    knows the data can judge it.
  * The Safe Harbor rule also requires that the holder of the data
    has no actual knowledge that the remaining information could be
    used to identify a person. Actual knowledge is not in the file,
    so no tool can check it.
  * A report with no violations found means only: none of the
    mechanical checks this tool can perform found a problem. It
    does not certify that a file meets HIPAA requirements, and it
    is not a legal determination of any kind.

ZIP LIST LIMIT: the bundled restricted-prefix list is the list published by the U.S. Department of Health and Human Services (guidance dated November 26, 2012), derived from Bureau of the Census 2000 Census ZIP Code Tabulation Areas. HHS warns in that guidance not to rely on the list if more current Census data is available, and no complete updated restricted list based on a later census was found published as of 2026-11-17. A ZIP prefix that is NOT in the bundled list is therefore reported as 'not flagged by the bundled 2000-Census list -- not verified against current Census data', never as verified safe.
